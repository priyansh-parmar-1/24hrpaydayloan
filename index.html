We connect you with lenders — we're not the lender.

24hrPaydayLoan is a referral service. When you submit a request, we share it with independent, participating lenders who may be able to offer you a short-term loan. You review any offer and decide whether to accept it — there's no obligation.

Built for people who need cash now.

Car trouble, a medical bill, a bill that landed at the wrong time — short-term loans exist to bridge the gap until your next paycheck. We keep the request process short so you're not stuck filling out paperwork when you're already stretched thin.

Who can apply?

Most lenders in our network look for applicants who are 18 or older, have a checking account with direct deposit, and receive regular income. Exact requirements vary by lender.

What does it cost?

We don't charge you anything to use this service. Any fees or interest come from the lender you're matched with, and will be disclosed to you before you agree to anything.
